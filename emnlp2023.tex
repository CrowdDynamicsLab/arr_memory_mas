\section*{Limitations}

\paragraph{Controlled synthetic setting.} The scenarios and downstream audiences are synthetic, so our leakage rates should not be read as production prevalence estimates. We use this design because the causal contrasts in E3--E9 require gold operational facts, gold boundary markers, and counterfactual variants that differ only in boundary representation; no existing public dataset combines these ingredients with multi-agent handoff structure. Production-trace validation remains future work.

\paragraph{Model coverage.} We evaluate two model families (\texttt{gpt-5-mini} and \texttt{deepseek-r1:32b}). Two families is replication, not broad coverage; whether the dose-response generalizes to other families and to smaller production-scale models is open.

\paragraph{Human validation and oracle E9.} The marker-survival judge ($\kappa = 0.74$ on a 100-item author-labeled sample) and the leakage detector are validated on targeted samples; larger independent annotation pools would strengthen semantic-leakage evaluation. E9 uses hand-constructed allowlists from gold annotations and is therefore an upper-bound existence probe, not an end-to-end mitigation: whether a generated handoff can carry such typed structure reliably is the engineering bridge we leave to future work. All scenarios are in English.
 
\section*{Ethics Statement}

All scenarios in the testbed are hand-authored synthetic vignettes; we use no real user data and no personally identifying information. We judge the net effect of disclosing summary collapse to be positive: it is a structural property of current LLM compression rather than a novel attack vector, and the operational-lifting probe points to a constructive direction. We release the testbed, evaluator, and analysis scripts so practitioners can audit their own systems. Experiments use inference only on \texttt{gpt-5-mini} and \texttt{deepseek-r1:32b}; no model weights are trained. Deployment-level claims about harm would require field studies we have not conducted; see~\Cref{app:ethics} for the full ethics discussion.
